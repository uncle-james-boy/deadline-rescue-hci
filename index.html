How we choose

Priority balances deadline urgency and the part of your grade this task supports, then adjusts for the time needed. Your day budget decides what fits.

Priority score = (assessment weight × task contribution × urgency factor) ÷ estimated hours.

Urgency rises as the due date gets closer. Overdue work is flagged. The highest-scoring unfinished task that fits your remaining time is suggested. You can always choose another.

This is a planning aid. Estimates and mark contribution are approximate; the score cannot predict your actual result.
